Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

Welcome to DigiLex. We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website and mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, update your profile, or interact with our features, we may collect personal details such as your username, password, profile picture, and application usage log (including your search history and bookmarked clauses).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to maintain your profile settings, save your personalized bookmark lists, provide dynamic search tracking features, and enhance overall system stability and application optimization.

3. Data Security and Protection

Your data security is our top priority. We implement industry-standard encryption protocols to safeguard your personal credentials from unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Your Rights and Account Deletion

You retain full control over your digital identity. You have the right to modify your username, avatar, and security credentials at any moment. Furthermore, you can permanently erase your records via the profile's danger zone, which completely removes your presence from our active directory.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions, security concerns, or legal inquiries regarding this disclosure framework, please reach out to us at support@digilex.com.
